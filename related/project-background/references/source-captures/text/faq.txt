What is Lumiq Studio?
+

We're a small studio designing objects and experiences at the meeting point of artificial intelligence, storytelling and craft.

Who is Lumiq for?
+

Anyone who wants tools that lift their imagination instead of crowding it — families, creators, readers and quiet thinkers.

Where do you ship?
+

We currently ship to most countries in North America, Europe and East Asia. More regions are coming soon.

How does the AI work?
+

AI is used as a collaborator, not a replacement. It helps narrate, illustrate and personalise — always with the person, not instead of them.

Is my data private?
+

Yes. We store as little as we can, never sell data, and offer offline modes for every product where it's possible.

Can I cancel a plan?
+

Any time. There are no contracts and no penalties.
